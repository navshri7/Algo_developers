OBJECTIVE:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Investigate relationships between different centrality measures to determine whether
they capture similar or complementary notions of importance in citation networks.

ALGORITHMS ANALYZED:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. K-Core Decomposition - Structural hierarchy
2. Betweenness Centrality - Path bottlenecks
3. Degree Centrality (In/Out/Total) - Direct connections
4. Bridging Centrality - Cross-community bridges
5. Katz Centrality - Influence propagation
6. Eigenvector Centrality - Hub importance
7. HITS - Hub and authority scores
8. PageRank - Random walk-based importance

CORRELATION METRICS USED:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Pearson Correlation: Linear relationship (parametric)
• Spearman Correlation: Rank-based relationship (non-parametric, robust to outliers)
• Kendall Tau: Concordance between rankings

INTERPRETATION & RECOMMENDATIONS:
====================================================================================================

SIMILAR IMPORTANCE NOTIONS (High Correlation):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• In-Degree and Authority Score: Both measure citation impact
• Betweenness and Bridging Centrality: Both identify bridge nodes
• Katz and Eigenvector: Both measure influence through connections

COMPLEMENTARY MEASURES (Low Correlation):
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• K-Core vs Betweenness: Structure vs. flow importance
• Degree vs Bridging Coefficient: Direct connections vs. bridge role
• Hub vs Authority: Different aspects of HITS algorithm

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR CITATION NETWORK ANALYSIS:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Use complementary measures together for comprehensive analysis
2. High-cited papers: Focus on In-Degree, Authority, Eigenvector
3. Frontier/Bridging papers: Focus on Bridging Centrality, Betweenness, Hub
4. Structural analysis: Use K-Core for community detection
5. Multi-perspective ranking: Combine multiple measures for robustness
